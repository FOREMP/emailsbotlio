Before you book
Do you take walk-ins?+
We're appointment-only, including for consultations. This lets us give every chair the full time it's booked for, without running behind.
How far in advance should I book colour?+
2–3 weeks is typical for balayage or correction work, especially on weekends. Cuts and toners can often be booked within the same week.
What if I need to reschedule?+
Just call or email at least 24 hours ahead and we'll move your appointment at no charge. Later changes may carry a small fee to cover the reserved time.
Do you offer bridal trials separately from the wedding day?+
Yes — we always recommend booking a trial 4–6 weeks before the date, so there's time to adjust before it matters.
