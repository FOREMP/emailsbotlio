GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers before you book.

Everything you need to know about our process, pricing, response times, and guarantees.

THE PROFIX PROMISE

No jargon. No guesswork. No surprises.

Every home and project is different, but our approach stays the same: listen first, explain clearly, and only recommend work that genuinely helps.

01
EMERGENCY SERVICE

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. A live member of our team answers emergency calls around the clock. We prioritize issues involving active leaks, loss of heat during extreme weather, electrical hazards, sewer backups, and other situations that cannot safely wait.

02
PRICING

How does your pricing work?

We begin with a diagnostic visit so our technician can understand the issue properly. Before any additional work starts, we provide a written estimate explaining the recommended solution and cost. There are no surprise charges for approved work.

03
APPOINTMENTS

How soon can someone come out?

Emergency calls are prioritized immediately. For standard appointments, we typically offer same-day or next-day availability, along with a clear arrival window and updates if our schedule changes.

04
CREDENTIALS

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes. ProFix is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. Our technicians receive ongoing training and hold relevant trade certifications, including NATE and EPA 608 credentials for qualified HVAC work.

05
WARRANTY

Do you guarantee your work?

We stand behind our craftsmanship. Repairs include a workmanship warranty, while installations include a longer coverage period depending on the system and manufacturer. We will explain the exact coverage before work begins.

06
SERVICE AREA

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Downtown, North Side, East End, West Valley, South Hills, Midtown, Riverside, Parkside, and surrounding neighborhoods. If you are unsure whether your address is covered, call us and we will confirm.

07
PREPARATION

How should I prepare for a service visit?

If possible, clear access to the equipment or area that needs attention. Make a note of unusual sounds, smells, error codes, or when the problem started. Photos can also help our team prepare before arrival.

08
PAYMENT

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, checks, and cash. For larger approved projects, financing options may be available. Our team will explain payment details clearly before work begins.
