About ProFix Services

Nearly two decades of keeping homes safe, comfortable, and running smoothly

Our Story: Built on Trust, Grown by Reputation

In 2005, Master Technician Mike Reynolds set out with a single work truck and a simple philosophy: treat every home like it's your own. After years of seeing contractors cut corners, overcharge, and leave homeowners frustrated, Mike knew there had to be a better way.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into the region's most trusted full-service HVAC, plumbing, and electrical company. Today, ProFix Services employs a team of over 40 certified technicians who share Mike's commitment to quality, honesty, and genuine customer care.

We've completed over 15,000 service calls and counting. Each one has taught us something new and reinforced what we already believe: that doing the right thing — every single time — is the only way to build a business that lasts.

Whether it's a midnight emergency call or a planned whole-home renovation, our team shows up ready to deliver exceptional results. We invest heavily in ongoing training, cutting-edge equipment, and a company culture that puts people first — both our customers and our employees.
